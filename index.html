CHRONICLES OF EXPLORATION

HISTORIC & FUTURE MISSIONS

Follow humanity's boldest voyages to uncharted worlds, from the Apollo lunar landings to outer solar system pioneers and the James Webb Space Telescope.

1969 • APOLLO 11

First Human Lunar Footstep

Destination: The Moon • Completed

Commander Neil Armstrong and Lunar Module Pilot Buzz Aldrin landed the Apollo Lunar Module Eagle on the Sea of Tranquility, fulfilling a historic human ambition.

1977 • VOYAGER 1 & 2

The Grand Tour of Outer Worlds

Destination: Interstellar Space • Active

Taking advantage of a rare planetary alignment, twin probes explored Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune before crossing the heliopause into interstellar space.

2004 • CASSINI-HUYGENS

Saturnian Ocean World Secrets

Destination: Saturn & Titan • Completed

Orbited Saturn for 13 years and deployed the ESA Huygens probe to land on Titan's hydrocarbon lakes, discovering active geothermal geysers erupting on Enceladus.

2012 • CURIOSITY & PERSEVERANCE

Search for Ancient Martian Biosignatures

Destination: Mars (Jezero Crater) • Active

Nuclear-powered rovers examining ancient lakebeds for prebiotic chemical building blocks and caching geological core samples for eventual return to Earth.

2021 • JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE

Piercing the Cosmic Dawn

Destination: Sun-Earth L2 Point • Active

The most powerful space observatory ever built, utilizing infrared optics with a 6.5-meter gold beryllium primary mirror to image galaxies formed just after the Big Bang.

2026–2030 • ARTEMIS PROGRAM

Permanent Lunar South Pole Base

Destination: Lunar South Pole • Planned

Establishing a permanent sustainable human presence on the Moon and building the Gateway orbital station as a staging platform for crewed missions to Mars.
