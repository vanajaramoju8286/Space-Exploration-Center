EXOPLANET TRANSIT & LIGHT CURVE ANALYSIS

When an exoplanet passes directly between its host star and an observer on Earth, it blocks a minute fraction of the star's light. Observing this periodic light dip reveals the exoplanet's orbital radius, diameter, and atmospheric transmission spectrum.
