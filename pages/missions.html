Filter and explore humanity's voyages into the unknown, from pioneering lunar landings to interplanetary orbiters, rovers, and future deep space missions.

Apollo 11

Destination: The Moon • Crewed Lunar Landing

The first crewed mission to land humans on the Moon, commanded by Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin aboard the Lunar Module Eagle.

Voyager 1 & 2

Destination: Outer Solar System & Beyond

Humanity's farthest emissaries, carrying the Golden Record across the heliopause and directly sampling interstellar space.

MISSION DOSSIER
COMPLETED 1997–2017

Cassini-Huygens

Destination: Saturn, Titan & Enceladus

Explored Saturn for 13 years, discovered hydrothermal vents on Enceladus, and soft-landed the European Huygens probe on Titan.

MISSION DOSSIER
ACTIVE ON MARS 2012–PRESENT

Mars Science Laboratory & M2020

Destination: Mars (Gale & Jezero Craters)

Heavy nuclear rovers drilling ancient Martian river deltas, confirming past habitable environments and caching sample tubes.

MISSION DOSSIER
ACTIVE • L2 ORBIT 2021–PRESENT

James Webb Space Telescope

Destination: Sun-Earth L2 (1.5M km)

Revolutionary infrared observatory with a 6.5m gold mirror peering back over 13.5 billion years to witness cosmic dawn.

MISSION DOSSIER
EXPEDITIONS PLANNED 2026–2030

Artemis II & III

Destination: Lunar South Pole & Gateway

Returning humanity to the Moon with Orion and Starship HLS, landing the first woman and person of color on the lunar south pole.
