HELIOCENTRIC ARCHITECTURE & ORBITAL MECHANICS

Our solar system was born 4.6 billion years ago from the gravitational collapse of a giant interstellar molecular cloud. Today, eight major planets orbit the central star on near-coplanar elliptical paths governed by Kepler's laws.
