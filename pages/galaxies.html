COSMIC MORPHOLOGY

THE ARCHITECTURE OF ISLAND UNIVERSES

Galaxies are vast gravitationally bound systems of stars, stellar remnants, interstellar gas, dust, and dark matter. The observable universe contains an estimated two trillion galaxies.

HUBBLE SEQUENCE: SA / SB

Spiral Galaxies

Characterized by a central bulge surrounded by a flat rotating disk of stars with logarithmic spiral arms. Rich in cold interstellar gas, driving ongoing star birth.

HUBBLE SEQUENCE: E0 - E7

Elliptical Galaxies

Smooth ellipsoidal profiles populated by elderly, low-mass stars with little to no interstellar dust. Often the product of multiple galactic mergers.

INTERSTELLAR MEDIUM

Diffuse Nebulae

Enormous clouds of hydrogen, helium, and ionized plasma glowing brilliantly under ultraviolet radiation from newborn O and B-type stars.

GRAVITATIONAL CORE

Globular Clusters

Dense, spherical concentrations of hundreds of thousands of ancient stars tightly bound by gravity, orbiting galactic cores like cosmic satellites.
