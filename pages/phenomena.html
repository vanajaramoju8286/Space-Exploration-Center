Supermassive Black Hole

Sagittarius A* Gravitational Singularity

A region of spacetime where gravity is so intense that nothing—not even light—can escape. Matter falling into the supermassive black hole forms a superheated, rapidly swirling accretion disk radiating energetic X-rays and relativistic particle jets.

ASTROPHYSICAL TELEMETRY
MASS EQUIVALENCE
4.15 Million Solar Masses
BOUNDARY RADIUS
12 Million km (Schwarzschild Radius)
PLASMA TEMPERATURE
